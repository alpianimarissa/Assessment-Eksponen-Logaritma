Solusi Logis

Ringkasan Sifat Eksponen & Logaritma

1. Sifat-sifat Eksponen (Pangkat)

$a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$
$\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$
$(a^m)^n = a^{m \cdot n}$
$(a \cdot b)^n = a^n \cdot b^n$
$a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$
$a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m}$

2. Bentuk Akar & Merasionalkan

$\sqrt{a \cdot b} = \sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b}$
$\frac{c}{\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}} = \frac{c(\sqrt{a}-\sqrt{b})}{a-b}$

3. Sifat-sifat Logaritma

$^a\log(b \cdot c) = ^a\log b + ^a\log c$
$^a\log(\frac{b}{c}) = ^a\log b - ^a\log c$
$^a\log(b^n) = n \cdot ^a\log b$
$^{a^m}\log(b^n) = \frac{n}{m} \cdot ^a\log b$
$^a\log b = \frac{^c\log b}{^c\log a} = \frac{1}{^b\log a}$
$^a\log b \times ^b\log c = ^a\log c$
